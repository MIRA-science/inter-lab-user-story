Evidence

G3BP1 is recruited before PABP1 during stress-granule assembly

Under arsenite stress, G3BP1 fluorescence intensity rose first; PABP1 intensity stayed flat, then climbed ~40 s later. The two markers do not arrive together — granule assembly is sequential, not simultaneous.
