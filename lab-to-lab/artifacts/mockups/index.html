MIRA × Discourse Graphs
INTER-LAB EXCHANGE OF RESULTS & REQUESTS · DRAFT v0.1

One lab's discovery, traveling into another lab's graph.

Sean has a result — two stress-granule proteins, imaged as the granule forms, one lighting up a beat before the other. Kate's lab wants it: not just the picture, but the experiment, the protocol, and a thread back to the 80 GB microscopy stack. And Kate has something to send back — “we need an experiment that doesn't exist yet.” These mockups show that exchange, in both directions, under permissions the producer controls — and for a lab that doesn't run a discourse-graph app at all, as a web page at a URL.
