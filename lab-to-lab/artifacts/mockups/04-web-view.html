G3BP1 is recruited to stress granules before PABP1

Under arsenite stress in HeLa cells, G3BP1 fluorescence intensity rose first; PABP1 intensity rose roughly 40 seconds later — consistent with sequential, not simultaneous, granule assembly. Measured by live lattice light-sheet imaging tracking both markers as granules form.
