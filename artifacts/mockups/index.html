shared dashboard → public

A result anyone can read in a browser — from a lab dashboard to the open web.

Sean, in the Vogel Lab, pushes a stress-granule result to a read-only shared dashboard his collaborator Kate's lab reads with no Discourse Graph app — glance-and-trust for the PI, traverse-to-the-method for her students, and a Request back for more. Take the same machinery fully public and it's Brian, at the Quantum Biology Institute, publishing to the whole world. Two worked examples, one spine: push a result to a shared web interface — trustworthy and addressable at a URL, readable without any DG tooling. The 🥉 public end is the inter-lab story's open-world sibling.

★ central use case6 SCREENS · 06–11

A producer pushes a selected subgraph to a read-only shared web interface — public, or password-protected for a consortium — that a known collaborator's lab navigates in a browser, with a Request-back current. v0.4 rebuilds every screen on the lab's real prepared data — a Lattice-SIM study of stress-granule composition over time. Grounded in the Vogel × Kate sessions and the user-story canvas.
