Your dashboard runs locally by default — it’s a local server (localhost:7979) on your machine, private and not reachable from outside. Going public means deliberately hosting it (e.g. on a server + a domain); that act — not a password — is what makes it public. Sharing to a person or consortium delivers nodes to their dashboard; sharing to the public option requires you to first host a public-facing instance.
