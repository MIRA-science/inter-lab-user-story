The magnetic-field effect in MFX-2 flips sign as the field strengthens

In MFX-2-bearing E. coli, the sign of the mean magnetic-field effect (MFE) flipped from positive to negative as magnetic field strength increased at low fields — positive near 0.5 mT, crossing zero, negative by ~2.5 mT. Measured by fieldscope live signal imaging across five field strengths.
