arsenite osmotic
illustrative · unpublished

Stress granules change composition over time — the two proteins go from excluding each other to co-localizing over the first ~10–15 min.

  • HeLa · sodium-arsenite and osmotic stress
  • The two stresses differ significantly in the first 10–15 min
  • Illustrative · unpublished — workshop prototype
Method & context
Cell lineHeLa
StressSodium arsenite · osmotic
ChannelsProtein 1–GFP (488) · Protein 2–mKate (561)
ImagingLattice SIM · ZEISS Elyra 7
ProtocolHeLa culture → stress → Lattice-SIM time-course → segment on per-pixel SUM → per-granule composition over time

Granules are segmented on the per-pixel SUM of both channels (not one), thresholded just above background — load-bearing: it defines every co-localization number that follows.
