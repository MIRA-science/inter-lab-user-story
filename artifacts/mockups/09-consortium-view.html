In plain language

for any reader

When cells are stressed, they bundle certain proteins into little droplets. This consortium found that one protein, G3BP1, shows up before another, PABP1, as a droplet forms — a consistent order. The board collects that finding, the experiments behind it, and what's still open.

Evidence (n live-imaging sessions, HeLa, two-channel): G3BP1 fluorescence onset precedes PABP1 by ~40 s during stress-granule assembly; order inverts on disassembly. Grounds → Study; follows → segmentation Protocol. Illustrative · unpublished — workshop prototype.
